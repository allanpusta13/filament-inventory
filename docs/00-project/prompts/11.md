## 📂 Phase 11: Immutable Audit Ledgers with Running Summarizers

### Developer Instruction Prompt
Configure the tracking ledgers under the **AUDIT LEDGERS** group, enforcing strict ledger immutability (omitting create, edit, or delete operations on their respective tables):

1. **`InTransitResource`**:
   * Displays virtual cargo currently in transit between physical warehouses.
   * Render columns for the dispatch reference code, origin/destination warehouses, variant SKU, dispatched quantity, and calculate the transit age in hours.
   * **The "Confirm Receipt" action**: Register an inline table action button `Confirm Receipt` inside the table. Launch a `2xl` wide dialog modal featuring a pre-populated repeater of item quantities. Clicking submit routes the inputs straight to `InventoryService::receiveRequisition()`, instantly clearing the transit row.
2. **`StockMovementResource`**:
   * Displays a dense log of physical events (receive, ship, transfer_in, transfer_out, adjustment, loss) with signed (+/-) indicators, color-coding actions to assist physical audits.
3. **`LossLedgerResource`**:
   * Renders shortage and damage write-offs with 4-decimal place price precision.
   * **The Aggregate Footer**: Within the `total_financial_loss` column, implement a customized `Sum` summarizer component to present a calculated aggregate write-off figure at the bottom of the table.
