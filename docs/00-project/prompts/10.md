# Prompt 10 — Current Stock View & Low Stock Widget

In my Laravel 13 + Filament v5 app, stock quantity is never stored directly —
it's always SUM(quantity) from stock_movements grouped by product_id and
warehouse_id. Build:

1. A Filament table/page (or a resource with a custom eloquent query) titled
   "Current Stock" that shows: product name, warehouse name, quantity
   (grouped SUM), built from a query like:
   StockMovement::query()->selectRaw('product_id, warehouse_id, SUM(quantity) as quantity')->groupBy('product_id','warehouse_id')
   Join in product name and warehouse name. Scope by role: non-admins only
   see rows for their assigned warehouses.

2. A Filament dashboard widget "Low Stock Alert" listing products where
   their current stock (per warehouse, or total — ask me which if unsure,
   default to per-warehouse) is at or below product.reorder_point. Scope
   this by role too.
